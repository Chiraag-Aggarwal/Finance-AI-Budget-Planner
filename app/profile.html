Privacy and your data

Finance AI stores your name, email, region, email-verification status, account creation date, and a salted password hash. It also stores sign-in sessions, password-reset and email-verification token hashes, and resend-limit details. Planner inputs and budget entries you save are linked to your account. Display preferences and language are stored in this browser.

The export contains your profile, saved planner information, budget entries, and any optional settings saved in the account database. It does not include your password hash or active sign-in and recovery tokens.

Deletion removes this account’s database records and clears its account-specific saved data in this browser. Browser-only preferences or unsynced drafts on other devices cannot be removed remotely.

This privacy summary is a product description, not a legal privacy policy. Professional review is required before public release.

Permanent action

Delete your account and all saved data?

This permanently removes your account profile, planner and budget data, account settings, sign-in sessions, and password-reset and verification records. You cannot undo this.
