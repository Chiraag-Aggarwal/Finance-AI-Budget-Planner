    How to read this plan

    Balance is your take-home income after the expenses and current EMIs listed above. Your emergency fund is money kept for unexpected costs. When a suitable loan is available, the completion estimate adds the time before purchase to the loan repayment period. The planner calculates a comfortable EMI limit from your monthly balance and selected savings share.

    How the calculations work
    • Monthly balance = take-home income − essential expenses − flexible expenses − current loan/EMI payments. A negative balance is shown as a shortfall.
    • Suggested monthly saving = positive balance × the share you selected. The rest is not counted as committed savings.
    • Emergency-fund target = (essential monthly expenses + current loan/EMI payments) × the number of months you selected. Your current fund is subtracted to show the remaining gap.
    • For a purchase goal, existing goal savings and only the assets or family support you choose to use count toward the planned contribution.
    • If loan comparison is on, EMI uses the annual rate you entered and a standard reducing-balance formula. A custom EMI limit is capped at your positive monthly balance; otherwise, the limit uses the share you selected.
    • With a loan, estimated completion time is the later of your target date or the time needed to save your planned contribution, plus the selected repayment term. Estimates assume costs and monthly saving stay the same; they exclude investment returns, price changes, fees, rate changes, and lender approval.

    Savings examples and starting forecasts use your current entries immediately. Forecasts are provisional; synthetic starter training is not evidence of real-world accuracy.
